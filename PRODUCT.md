## Platform

web

## Users
Software engineers preparing for senior roles or system-design interviews, and architects who want worked examples. They study in focused sessions on a laptop, often in the evening.

## Product Purpose
ArchitectIQ teaches large-scale system design: a project library of case studies (Netflix, YouTube, Uber, WhatsApp; Beginner to Enterprise), an interactive architecture canvas (drag nodes like CDN, load balancer, gateway, services, databases, queues and connect them), a learning hub (consistent hashing, sharding, CAP, circuit breakers), a community forum with events, a gamified dashboard with badges, and an admin analytics view.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single `architectiq.html` with inline CSS/JS; pages toggled in place. A FastAPI backend exists in `backend/`.

## Brand Commitments
- Name "ArchitectIQ" (the "IQ" was styled separately).

## Evidence on Hand
- Case studies, community posts, badges and admin numbers are demo content inside the HTML. Do not add claims.

## Product Principles
1. Show the architecture: diagrams explain faster than paragraphs.
2. Progress you can see, without arcade gimmicks.
3. Serious, precise, a little playful.
